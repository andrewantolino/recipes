# Bang Bang Chicken Salad

Sliced soy-garlic-ginger chicken breast over rocket, roasted sweet potato and crunchy chickpeas, with a creamy, spicy peanut bang bang dressing.

- **Serves:** 2
- **Prep:** 20 min (plus 1-4 hours marinating)
- **Cook:** about 35 min
- **Oven:** chicken at 180°C, vegetables at 210°C

## Chicken marinade

- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (reduced-salt if available)
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (or ½ tsp ground)
- 1 tbsp oil (a splash of sesame oil is good)
- 1 tsp rice vinegar (or juice of ½ lemon)
- Pinch of chilli flakes
- A few grinds of black pepper

No extra salt needed. The soy covers it.

## Bang bang dressing

- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1-2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha or chilli oil, to taste
- 1 tsp rice vinegar (or juice of ½ lemon), to taste
- 1-2 tbsp warm water, to loosen to a drizzle
- Optional: 1 small grated garlic clove, splash of sesame oil

## Salad

- 1 large sweet potato, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Oil, for roasting
- Smoked paprika or chilli flakes (optional, for the chickpeas)
- Rocket
- 2-3 spring onions (or white onion), thinly sliced
- Small handful Turkish roasted pumpkin seeds (salty), roughly chopped
- Splash of rice vinegar and a drizzle of oil for the rocket (or a little dressing)

## Method

1. **Marinate.** Mix the marinade ingredients and coat the chicken. Refrigerate for 1-4 hours, turning once.
2. **Prep the chickpeas.** Dry them well in a clean tea towel and let them air-dry for a few minutes. Toss with oil (and paprika or chilli if using). Skip the salt, since the pumpkin seeds and dressing are salty.
3. **Roast the vegetables.** Heat the oven to 210°C. Toss the sweet potato with oil and salt and roast about 25 minutes until caramelised at the edges. Put the chickpeas on their own tray and roast 25-35 minutes, shaking once or twice, until deep golden and hard.
4. **Cook the chicken.** Wipe most of the marinade off. Place in a covered dish and bake at 180°C for 25-30 minutes, until 74°C in the thickest part. If one end is thicker, flatten the breast first so it cooks evenly. Rest covered in the dish for 10 minutes.
5. **Make the dressing.** Whisk everything together and taste. Adjust the vinegar, sweet chilli and heat to balance sweet, spicy, salty and creamy.
6. **Toast the pumpkin seeds.** Dry pan, no oil, medium heat, single layer, 2-3 minutes, moving constantly. Tip onto a plate straight away and roughly chop. They crisp as they cool.
7. **Prep the spring onions.** Soak the slices in iced water for 5-10 minutes, then drain.
8. **Slice the chicken** against the grain, about 1 cm thick, on a slight diagonal. Spoon over a little of the dish juices.

## Assembly

1. Toss the rocket with a splash of rice vinegar and a drizzle of oil (or a little dressing).
2. Add the warm sweet potato.
3. Fan the sliced chicken over the top.
4. Drizzle with the dressing and keep extra on the side.
5. Finish with the crunchy chickpeas, pumpkin seeds and spring onions.

## Notes

- Add the chickpeas last so the dressing doesn't soften them.
- The pumpkin seeds are a garnish, not a component. They are very salty, so use a small handful.
- Don't marinate much past 4 hours with the acid in. Without the vinegar, longer is fine.
- Lemon or lime juice can replace the rice vinegar in both the marinade and the dressing, at about ½ lemon each.
- Yoghurt variant for the chicken: 3 tbsp plain yoghurt, 1 tbsp oil, 2 grated garlic cloves, juice of ½ lemon, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cumin, a good pinch of salt.
