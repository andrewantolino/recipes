# Korean-Inspired Bang Bang Chicken Salad

Cover-baked shredded chicken breast in a gochujang and peanut butter marinade, tossed with a peanut dressing and crunchy veg. Makes about 4-5 serves.

**Scaling rule:** this recipe is built around 1 kg of chicken breast. Double everything for 2 kg, halve everything for 500 g.

## Ingredients (for 1 kg chicken)

### Chicken and marinade

- 1 kg chicken breast (about 4 breasts)
- 40 g gochujang (about 2 tbsp)
- 35 g smooth peanut butter (about 2 tbsp)
- 15 ml soy sauce (1 tbsp)
- 15 ml sesame oil (1 tbsp)
- 15 ml rice vinegar or lime juice (1 tbsp)
- 5 g fresh ginger, grated (about 1 tsp)
- 6 g garlic, minced (about 2 cloves)
- 30-50 ml warm water, to loosen into a spreadable paste
- Optional: 5 g honey or maple syrup (1 tsp) for extra sweetness

### Dressing

- 70 g smooth peanut butter (about 4 tbsp)
- 30 ml lime juice (2 tbsp)
- 30 ml soy sauce (2 tbsp)
- 10 ml sesame oil (2 tsp)
- 10 g honey or maple syrup (2 tsp)
- 40-60 ml warm water, to thin
- Optional: 1 tbsp gochujang or chili oil, for extra heat

### Salad

- 300 g cabbage, finely shredded
- 150 g carrot (about 2), julienned or grated
- 250 g cucumber (about 1), sliced into thin batons
- 4 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 20 g fresh coriander, roughly chopped
- 40 g roasted peanuts, crushed
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

**Per 100 g of chicken (for odd amounts):** 4 g gochujang, 3.5 g peanut butter, 1.5 ml soy, 1.5 ml sesame oil, 1.5 ml rice vinegar. Dressing is roughly 7 g peanut butter, 3 ml lime juice, 3 ml soy, 1 ml sesame oil, 1 g honey per 100 g of chicken.

**Optional, to nudge it closer to Sichuan bang bang chicken:**
- Swap the rice vinegar for Chinese black vinegar (or use half and half)
- Add 10 g tahini or Chinese sesame paste to the dressing
- Finish with chili oil or chili crisp
- Add a pinch of ground Sichuan pepper for the tingle

## Method

1. **Preheat.** Heat the oven to 180°C (fan-forced 160°C).
2. **Make the marinade.** In a bowl, whisk together the gochujang, peanut butter, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic and honey (if using). Add warm water a little at a time until it's a thick but spreadable paste.
3. **Coat the chicken.** Place the chicken breasts in a baking dish and rub the marinade over all sides.
4. **Cover and bake.** Cover tightly with a lid or foil. Bake for 35-45 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 74°C. For easier shredding, take it to 80°C or a few minutes beyond.
5. **Rest.** Leave the chicken covered in the dish for 10 minutes so it reabsorbs its juices.
6. **Shred.** Transfer the chicken to a stand mixer with the paddle attachment and mix on low until shredded (about 20-30 seconds). Alternatively, use two forks.
7. **Toss in the juices.** Return the shredded chicken to the dish and toss it in the pan juices.
8. **Make the dressing.** Whisk the dressing ingredients together, adding warm water until it's pourable.
9. **Assemble.** Combine the chicken, cabbage, carrot, cucumber, spring onion and coriander. Toss with the dressing. Top with crushed peanuts and sesame seeds.

## Serving

- Serve cold or at room temperature as a salad.
- Also works as a wrap or rice bowl filling.
- Add a drizzle of chili oil or extra gochujang on top for more heat.

## Notes

- **Skip the extra salt.** Gochujang and soy are both salty, so taste before adding any.
- **Splitting the flavour:** for a lighter result, bake the chicken with just the gochujang, soy, sesame oil and ginger, and keep all the peanut butter for the dressing.
- **Use the pan juices.** They're concentrated flavour, so always toss the shredded chicken in them before dressing.
- **Garlic:** the marinade is cooked straight away, so the garlic-in-oil storage concern doesn't apply. If you marinate ahead, keep it in the fridge for a few hours at most, or freeze it.
- **Fridge life:** dressed salad is best eaten the same day, as the cabbage and cucumber release water. Undressed shredded chicken keeps 3-4 days in an airtight container at or below 4°C. Keep the dressing and veg separate until serving. Cooked chicken also freezes for 2-3 months.
- Conversion basis: 1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 tsp = 5 ml. Peanut butter is ~17 g per tbsp, gochujang ~20 g per tbsp.
