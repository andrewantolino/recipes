# Chinese Satay Chicken Stir Fry

Source: https://www.recipetineats.com/chinese-satay-chicken-stir-fry/
(Nagi Maehashi, RecipeTin Eats)

Takeaway-style satay. Less coconut and peanut than Thai/Malaysian satay,
stronger on the spice blend.

Scaled 2x from the original (250g chicken, serves 2). Quantities are in
grams, with the spices pre-mixed in a jar (recipe at the bottom).

- Serves: 4 (excluding rice)
- Prep: 15 min
- Cook: 10 min
- Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 500g boneless skinless chicken thigh (breast: see Notes)

### Satay sauce

- 19g satay spice mix (see "Satay spice mix" below)
- 50g smooth peanut butter
- 16g brown sugar
- 12g light soy sauce
- 20g rice vinegar
- 20g sriracha (or other hot sauce)
- 5g lemongrass paste
- 160g full fat coconut milk
- 120g water

### Stir fry

- 25g peanut oil (or other plain oil)
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- Sliced shallots / scallions, to garnish (optional)

To serve: steamed rice.

## Method

### 1. Prep

Trim excess fat from the chicken and slice into thin bite-size strips.

### 2. Sauce

Weigh everything except the water into a bowl: spice mix, peanut butter,
sugar, soy, vinegar, sriracha, lemongrass paste and coconut milk. Mix,
then stir in the water.

### 3. Stir fry

Use a very hot wok and cook the chicken in two batches if the pan is
small. Crowding steams it instead of browning.

1. Heat the oil in a wok or skillet over high heat. Add onion and garlic.
   Stir fry for 1 minute.
2. Add the chicken. Stir fry for 3-4 minutes, until just cooked through
   and a bit browned.
3. Drop the heat slightly and pour in the sauce. Stir fry until reduced
   and thickened, 1 1/2 to 2 minutes.
4. Serve straight away over rice. Garnish with shallots if using.

## Satay spice mix

Makes 5 batches of the recipe above (about 95g). Measure with level
spoons so the ratios match the original recipe.

- 5 tsp coriander powder
- 5 tsp cumin powder
- 2 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 1/2 tsp chilli powder (pure ground chilli, not "chili powder" blend)
- 15 tsp curry powder
- 5 tsp salt
- 2 1/2 tsp white pepper

Mix well and store in an airtight jar away from light. Best used within
about 6 months.

Per batch: 19g is an estimate (7 1/2 tsp of mix). Weigh the finished jar,
divide by 5, and update the 19g in the ingredients to match.

## Notes

- Using chicken breast: velvet it first so it stays tender. Slice 500g
  breast thinly, toss with 7g baking soda and leave for 20 minutes. Rinse
  well and pat dry with paper towel. Cook for 2-3 minutes instead of 3-4.
- Chilli powder: in the US, "chili powder" is a spice blend. Use pure
  ground chilli, or cayenne pepper.
- Curry powder: any basic one works.
- Soy sauce: light or all-purpose. Avoid dark soy, too intense.
- Lemongrass paste (tube or jar) stirs straight in. Fresh lemongrass would
  need very fine chopping and sauteing first.
- Heat: the chilli powder and sriracha are easy to halve or drop.
- Commenters add veg: carrot, broccoli, mushrooms, cauliflower. Cook the
  veg with the onion, remove, then add back after the chicken has browned.
- Nutrition: about 484 kcal per serving at the original 2 serves
  (excluding rice, made with breast). Roughly the same per 250g of
  chicken here, a bit higher with thigh.
- Gram weights for liquids and paste are approximate conversions from the
  original spoon measures. Adjust to taste the first time.
