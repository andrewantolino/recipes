# Gyudon-Style Beef Udon with Shiitake, Rocket and Soft Egg

A brothy riff on gyudon: beef, onion and shiitake simmered in beef stock, over chewy udon, finished with wilted rocket, a runny-set egg and spring onions. Works over rice too (see notes).

- **Serves:** 2
- **Time:** ~25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 460g frozen udon (2 x 230g blocks)
- 275g thinly sliced beef (shaved scotch fillet or hotpot slices)
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- 120g fresh shiitake, stems removed, caps sliced
- 600ml water
- 1 beef stock cube
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 60g rocket
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- Black pepper, plus shichimi or pickled ginger to serve (optional)

## Method

1. **Cook the udon.** Boil the frozen udon in a separate pot for 3 minutes from frozen. Pull them with a very slight hardness left in the centre, as they finish cooking to a perfect chew once the hot broth is spooned over. Drain and divide between two bowls. Cooking them in the broth makes it starchy.
2. **Simmer the broth.** Combine water, stock cube, 2 tbsp soy, mirin, sugar and ginger in a pot. Add the onion and shiitake and simmer gently for about 9 minutes, until the onion is soft and sweet and the mushrooms are tender.
3. **Add the beef.** Drop in the slices, separating them as they go in. Simmer about 1 minute, just until no longer pink. Skim any scum off the top. Don't overcook.
4. **Set the eggs.** Bring the broth to barely a simmer, then pour in the beaten eggs in a slow, thin stream while swirling. Turn off the heat, cover, and wait 45-50 seconds. The outside should be just set and the middle still runny. Err on the underdone side, as the egg keeps cooking in the hot broth.
5. **Wilt the rocket.** Pile it on top and push it under the broth. Leave 20-30 seconds in the residual heat. Don't put the heat back on.
6. **Assemble.** Ladle the beef, egg, mushrooms, rocket and broth over the udon. Top with spring onions and pepper, plus shichimi or pickled ginger if you like.

## Notes

- **Rice instead of udon:** skip step 1 and ladle everything over steamed rice (about 200g cooked short-grain rice per bowl). For a more traditional, less soupy donburi, use a bit less broth.
- One 230g block per bowl is generous because frozen udon puffs up. Cook a third block if feeding 3 or very hungry.
- If the broth tastes thin, add a splash more soy or a tiny pinch of salt rather than another cube.
- A splash of rice vinegar or some pickled ginger at the end cuts through the richness.

## Variations

- **Dashi version:** replace the 600ml water with 600ml dashi and use half a stock cube (or none). Start with 1 tbsp soy, since dashi powder and cubes are also salty.
- **Deeper mushroom flavour:** soak 2-3 dried shiitake in the water for 20 minutes beforehand and use the soaking liquid as part of the broth.
